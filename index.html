How to play

Goal: clear the plaza before the horn. Your multiplier builds as you knock rioters down and send them running. At the horn you receive play amount × multiplier in Coins.

  • Drag in the bottom pad to aim the cannon. Up = farther.
  • Hold to spray. Watch the pressure gauge. Let go to refill.
  • Knock people into each other for chain bonuses.
  • Shields block the front. Hit them from an angle or knock someone into them.
  • Throwers pelt the truck. Every hit costs you.
  • The gas officer backs you up with tear gas.
  • Tap SCOPE for binoculars: zoom in on the crowd and spot throwers, shields and heavies. You can keep spraying while you look.
